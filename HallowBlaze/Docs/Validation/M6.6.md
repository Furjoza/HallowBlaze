## Human Protocol Version 1

Recruit **5-8 distinct people**, anonymous `P01` through `P08`, excluding the implementer and
moderator from the evaluable sample. Record prior rules/landmark exposure, language, actual
revision and display size. Aim for 15-20 minutes each; permit stopping without penalty.

Freeze this protocol and the tested revision before recruiting. Use variant orders
`base/east-guard/north-push`, `east-guard/north-push/base`, `north-push/base/east-guard`,
`base/north-push/east-guard`, `north-push/east-guard/base`, `east-guard/base/north-push`
for P01-P06 respectively, repeating the first two for P07-P08. First cases give all variants
first-contact coverage; later cases are learned observations and must not be pooled with them.
Use fresh isolated boundaries for every attempt, not the state another person left behind.

1. Show only the live starting board. Explain existing controls/legend consistently if needed,
   recording all assistance, but give no route, push direction, correct outcome or solution sequence.
2. Read the neutral prompt: "You have reached the radio tower. Choose the way through that you
   would use in a run. Before acting, say what you expect to gain, spend or risk, and why."
3. Lock the intended result, planned commands or route, predicted Food/HP/charge implications,
   and causal explanation before executing. Record uncertainty verbatim; do not coach it away.
4. Let the participant execute ordinary controls. Record accepted/rejected commands, actual
   resource changes, final result/death, assistance and any divergence. A rejection is free.
5. Before showing an answer key, ask: "What caused that result? What would you change, and
   what other result would you consider?" Lock the explanation and alternative tradeoff.
6. Repeat with the next fresh variant without solution instructions. Preserve the first-contact
   sheet; later insight cannot repair its score. After all attempts are locked, discuss surprises.

### Scoring And Decision

Score from the locked plan, actual event/resource record and post-attempt explanation, not from
successful arrival alone. Report each variant and phase separately, with raw denominators.

- **Consequence explanation passes** when the participant correctly identifies their actual
  outcome or failure, accounts for the meaningful action/Food and tool-charge costs of the route
  they actually took, and explains relevant pressure/gate or retained-enemy consequences without
  hidden randomness. Record exact numerical prediction error separately; a wrong first prediction
  must remain wrong even when the later explanation is correct.
- **Tradeoff explanation passes** when the participant distinguishes reaching the tower from
  taking the optional supplies and explains a concrete extra cost/risk or manual-versus-tool
  tradeoff. Merely repeating an outcome label or reaching an exit by luck is insufficient.
- **Proceed:** 5-8 evaluable completed sessions, at least one evaluable first-contact record per
  variant, a strict majority of participants passing both explanation criteria on their first case,
  variant-specific results reviewed, and no reproduced technical contradiction or mandatory
  softlock. For 5/6/7/8 participants, the majority is 3/4/4/5. The owner records the actual decision;
  a threshold alone does not automatically close M6 or the separate M4.9 gate.
- **Revise:** technical behavior is valid but explanation criteria fail. Classify UI, rule or
  tutorial causes, preserve evidence and propose a separately scoped repair before another cohort.
- **Stop:** reproducible rules/presentation mismatch, mandatory softlock or unsafe/unusable
  interactive setup. Preserve the exact variant/address/commands and resolve the blocker first.
- **Pending:** fewer than five reliable completed sessions, missing variant coverage or missing
  locked records. Missing observations are not a zero-percent result or a Proceed waiver.

Log technical invalidations/exclusions with reasons and report recruited, completed, excluded
and evaluable totals. Never drop misunderstandings or silently combine a post-repair cohort.
Do not store names, contact details or private player files in the repository.
