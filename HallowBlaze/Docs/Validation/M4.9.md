## Recruitment And Session Order

Recruit **5-8 distinct people**, using anonymous IDs `P01` through `P08`. Record prior exposure
to this game/rules and exclude implementers/moderators from the primary participant denominator.
Use fresh boards per case, identical viewport/zoom, muted audio and animations disabled.
Do not show the moderator answer key or outcome images before a response is committed.
Record the actual baseline, fixture revision, viewport and any display/accessibility changes.

1. First contact: show the unhinted case cards. State the planned player command or the choice
  set, but do not name the expected enemy action, point at a symbol or explain a rule. Collect
  and lock predictions for **all nine cards before revealing any outcome**, preventing feedback
  on an earlier case from teaching the later first-contact cases.
2. Before revealing an outcome, record exact predicted enemy action and cell, expected HP
   change, predicted noise target where applicable, chosen command and a short explanation.
   Record uncertainty rather than converting it into a correct answer. Lock this response.
3. After the complete first-contact sheet is locked, reveal the selected outcomes. Read each
  actual action/cell/HP change from the moderator key; a still after image alone cannot show
  an attack that missed. Record surprise and the participant's explanation separately; no
  correction changes the original score.
4. Give every participant the same short familiarization script below. Record extra assistance.
5. Learned phase: use the matched second form, not the already revealed first-contact board.
  Again lock the whole sheet before any reveal, with no further hints. Counterbalance form order by ID:
   odd IDs use A then B, even IDs use B then A. Keep first-contact and learned scores separate.
6. Ask the participant to choose an action that intentionally redirects the Listener, say where
   it will go and when, then reveal that branch. Distinguish goal-directed reasoning from a
   lucky choice; choosing the right command without explaining its effect is not a lure success.
7. Finish with a short explanation of any failure using visible intent/noise and fixed rules.
   Capture ambiguity and moderator assistance, not names or personal data.

Suggested duration: 15-20 minutes per participant. Stop a session on request or technical failure.
Do not discard a misunderstood case or participant to improve the score. Record exclusions and
technical invalidations with reasons; retain raw denominators and do not silently replace records.

## Standard Familiarization

Moderator reads the following meaning consistently in the participant's language. Record the
language and any deviation; never improvise extra coaching for one participant.

"The marker announces an enemy's next opportunity. The movement arrow with its attack badge
means it attacks only if you enter that exact destination; another occupied destination blocks it.
A planned attack stays on its announced cell even if you leave. Pause means Wait. Shamblers
alternate an active opportunity and rest. The magnifier marks a Listener's next investigation
step; the linked diamond is the heard noise source, not another movement this turn. A Listener
remembers the newest noise it actually heard, not necessarily the loudest, and does not chase
the source actor. A new noise affects its next plan, not an already announced step. Expired noise
or a blocked step causes Wait, without another route or hidden attack. Noise outlines show the
same hearing area used by the rules. Strength is the path budget; remaining lifetime decreases
only after accepted commands. Wait is silent, movement uses strength 2, interaction strength 4."

Do not read this script during first contact. Do not reveal a particular case's correct command.

## Measures And Decision Rule

Freeze the case list, answer key and scoring before the first participant. The following are
operational criteria for this small diagnostic, not a statistical population estimate.

- A prediction is correct only when the enemy action, actual cell and HP consequence match
  the selected command's outcome. Investigation cases also require distinguishing the immediate
  step from the announced noise source and predicting the next announced investigation source.
  A next Wait has no announced source even if memory still exists at an already reached source;
  do not score these as the same question. Report counts per case and per person, not only a pooled percentage.
- A learned participant passes archetype prediction with at least 3 of 4 Shambler cases and
  3 of 4 Listener cases correct, including the same-cell conditional attack and locked/noise case.
- First-contact comprehension scores are diagnostic only: they have no pass threshold, are not
  pooled into learned results and do not by themselves trigger Proceed or Revise. Technical
  invalidation remains actionable regardless of the phase in which it occurs.
- Case 09 presents a goal and two candidate sequences. Deliberate luring passes only when the
  participant selects **Interact with F, then Wait**, predicts Wait at the original enemy cell
  after the first command and movement to the goal after the second, predicts zero HP loss, and
  explains the connection to audible noise rather than player pursuit. Score **only this chosen
  sequence**; predicting the unchosen Wait/Wait branch is not required. A correct prediction of
  choosing Wait/Wait does not achieve the lure goal. Record first-contact and learned lure results
  separately; only the learned attempt contributes to the Proceed comprehension thresholds.
- **Proceed:** 5-8 completed evaluable participants; a strict majority of the full evaluable sample
  passes learned Shambler prediction, learned Listener prediction and deliberate luring separately;
  no demonstrated intent/execution mismatch; failure explanations require no hidden randomness.
- **Revise:** technical correctness holds but any comprehension criterion fails. Classify the
  failure and propose a separate scoped UI/rule/tutorial repair; do not patch production in M4.9.
- **Stop:** a reproducible intent/execution contradiction or unusable test setup prevents fair
  assessment. Preserve the evidence and open the blocking issue before another cohort.
- **Pending:** fewer than five completed evaluable sessions or no reliable pre-reveal records.
  Missing evidence is not a zero-percent result and cannot be labeled Proceed.

For 5/6/7/8 evaluable participants, a strict majority is 3/4/4/5 respectively. Record technical
invalidations separately and show recruited, completed, excluded and evaluable totals. A second
cohort after repairs has its own baseline/results; never merge it into the first cohort silently.

## Error Classification

| Code | Primary category | Evidence to capture |
| --- | --- | --- |
| UI | Symbol, source/step association, overlap or range unreadable | Visible marker/viewport and the participant's interpretation |
| Rule | The visible information was understood but its rule was mispredicted | Recorded rule explanation versus deterministic actual outcome |
| Tutorial | The standard explanation failed or extra instruction was required | Exact assistance, timing and whether the learned case was affected |
| Technical | Capture, display or resolution did not match the pinned fixture | Case/form/command, artifact path and reproducible mismatch |

Assign one primary category with evidence, optional secondary categories, and confidence in the
classification. Do not call a UI error a participant failure without recording what was visible.
Technical invalidation is not an incorrect human prediction and cannot be counted as a success.
